Golden set review

What counts as a claim
  • risk: a specific risk the company says could hurt it. One claim per distinct risk; each item of a risk list is its own claim. A sentence that only restates a consequence is not a new claim.
  • guidance: a forward-looking target, plan or expectation about the company's own results, spending, timing or exposure, including "we do not expect..." statements. Strategy with no target, amount or date is not guidance.
  • metric: a reported number about the company's results or its operating environment (e.g. benchmark prices). Dates alone are not metrics.
  • event: a completed or announced corporate action: acquisition, divestiture, offering, dividend or buyback change, restructuring, impairment.
  • Never: disclaimers, generic boilerplate, definitions, cross-references, lists of laws. A heading is never a claim on its own, but a risk-factor heading that states a labeled risk is an alternative quote for it (dashed underline).
  • Quote: the clause that states the claim, exactly as filed, at most ~40 words. Add an alternative quote when the claim is also stated elsewhere.
